7 Best European VPS Providers in 2026

Uptime guarantees, hardware specs, GDPR compliance, pricing, and support compared across Hetzner, Atal Networks, OVHcloud, UpCloud, Vultr, Contabo, and Hostinger.

Likhon Ahmed
Updated August 2026 • 3,800 words

The best European VPS provider depends on what you need most. For a formal uptime SLA with service credits and EU data residency, Atal Networks ranks first. For raw price-to-hardware value, Hetzner leads. For enterprise DDoS protection and GPU infrastructure, OVHcloud wins. This guide compares 7 providers across the criteria that actually matter in production: uptime guarantees, hardware specs, GDPR compliance, pricing, and support.

What Separates a Good European VPS From a Great One

Not all EU VPS providers are equal. Four factors determine real-world quality.

NVMe storage: NVMe delivers 3 to 7 times the throughput of SATA SSD. In 2026, any serious European VPS provider should offer NVMe as standard. HDD-backed plans are not suitable for production workloads.

Formal SLA with service credits: A 99.9% uptime claim without published service credits is a marketing statement. A formal SLA with service credits means the provider pays you when uptime drops below the threshold. Only two providers on this list carry a formal SLA with service credits.

GDPR and the CLOUD Act: Hosting in the EU satisfies GDPR data residency by default. The CLOUD Act matters for one additional reason: US-headquartered companies must provide user data to US authorities upon request, even when data sits inside EU data centers. Vultr is the only US-headquartered provider on this list. All others are European-headquartered and subject to EU law only.

7 Best European VPS Providers in 2026

1

Hetzner Best Price-to-Hardware Value in Europe

Hetzner is a German provider founded in 1997 with ISO 27001 and C5 Type 2 certified data centers in Nuremberg, Falkenberg, and Helsinki. Three price increases hit in 2026, with the April adjustment raising all cloud products 30% to 37%. The CX23 now starts at €5.49/month (~$6.20 USD), up from €3.79/month pre-April 2026. Even post-increase, Hetzner includes 20 TB of monthly transfer, which no comparable EU provider matches at this price.

Starting from: €5.49/month (~$6.20 USD, CX23)

Pros:

  • German-owned, subject to EU law only, no CLOUD Act
  • ISO 27001 and C5 Type 2 certified
  • 20 TB monthly transfer is included even after price increases
  • NVMe storage, AMD EPYC, and Intel CPU options
  • Strong API, Terraform, and Ansible support

Cons:

  • Three 2026 price increases raised the CX23 from €3.79 to €5.49/month
  • No formal uptime SLA with service credits on standard cloud plans
  • Ticket-only support, no phone or live chat
  • No Windows OS on standard cloud plans

Best for: Developers and technical teams that need EU data sovereignty, strong bandwidth allocation, and price-to-hardware value without formal SLA requirements.

2

Atal Networks Best Overall European VPS

Atal Networks delivers KVM VPS hosting across EU locations, including Germany, France, the Netherlands, and the UK, built on Dell hardware with Intel Xeon processors, NVMe SSD storage, and network ports from 1Gbps to 10Gbps. The platform has served 35,000+ businesses across 196 countries from 213+ data centers over 15+ years.

Every plan includes full root access, BYOIP, Custom ISO, DDoS protection, and 24/7/365 engineer support. Payments cover crypto, PayPal, credit card, and bank transfer.

Starting from: $5.21/month

Pros:

  • Only provider on this list with a formal 99.99% uptime SLA backed by a 100% guarantee with service credits
  • Linux and Windows VPS from multiple EU locations in one product line
  • BYOIP and Custom ISO for VPN operators and network-heavy deployments
  • Crypto payments accepted; no setup fees; no hidden charges
  • 24/7/365 engineer support, not a ticket queue

Cons:

  • Some EU deployments run through partner-led infrastructure, not wholly owned Atal Networks facilities
  • Entry pricing sits above Hetzner for teams that do not need a formal SLA

Best for: SaaS platforms, FinTech, VPN operators, gaming servers, and enterprises that need a documented uptime guarantee with service credits across EU locations.

3

OVHcloud Best for DDoS Protection and Enterprise EU VPS

OVHcloud is Europe's largest cloud provider, French-headquartered, with 500,000+ servers across 46 data centers. Its 2026 hardware refresh added AMD EPYC 9005 dual-socket configurations. The in-house VAC DDoS mitigation protects every plan without sinkholes, critical for gaming and public platforms where any downtime costs revenue. Public Cloud Instances carry a 99.95% uptime SLA. Private Cloud solutions carry up to 99.99%.

Starting from: ~€4.99/month (VPS Value, post-April 2026 pricing)

Pros:

  • French-owned, subject to EU law only, no CLOUD Act
  • In-house VAC DDoS mitigation does not sinkhole attack IPs
  • 2026 EPYC 9005 hardware refresh
  • 500 GB backup storage included on VPS plans
  • Formal SLA: 99.95% Public Cloud, up to 99.99% Private Cloud

Cons:

  • April 2026 price increases raised EU tier costs
  • Control panel complexity slows smaller teams
  • Budget tier customers receive limited support access

Best for: Gaming platforms, regulated enterprises, and high-traffic public-facing applications where DDoS protection and EU data sovereignty are non-negotiable.

4

UpCloud Best for Benchmark Performance and Finnish EU Sovereignty

UpCloud is a Finnish cloud provider founded in Helsinki in 2012, operating European data centers in Frankfurt, Amsterdam, London, Warsaw, Madrid, and Helsinki. Its proprietary MaxIOPS storage consistently tops independent VPS benchmarks. Starter plans launched in May 2026 at €3/month. Premium plans carry a 99.999% uptime SLA.

Starting from: €3/month (Starter, May 2026)

Pros:

  • Finnish-owned, subject to EU law only, no CLOUD Act
  • MaxIOPS proprietary storage ranks near the top in independent benchmark testing
  • 99.999% uptime SLA on premium plans
  • Six EU city data centers
  • Managed Kubernetes, managed MySQL, and PostgreSQL databases
  • Hourly billing available

Cons:

  • Helsinki data center carries a 1.5x price premium versus other EU locations
  • Outbound traffic billed at $0.01/GB beyond the free tier
  • The managed services catalog is smaller than DigitalOcean or Vultr

Best for: Performance-sensitive SaaS platforms, database-heavy applications, and developers who need top-tier disk I/O with the strongest uptime documentation on this list.

5

Vultr Best for Multi-City EU Coverage

Vultr operates nine European data center cities: Amsterdam, Frankfurt, London, Paris, Warsaw, Madrid, Stockholm, Bucharest, and Milan. No other provider on this list matches that geographic range across Europe. Cloud Compute starts at $2.50/month. High Frequency NVMe instances start around $6/month with strong single-core clock speeds for latency-sensitive workloads.

Note: Vultr is US-headquartered. The CLOUD Act applies to data in its EU data centers.

Starting from: $2.50/month (Cloud Compute Regular, EU)

Pros:

  • Nine European city data centers, the broadest EU geographic coverage on this list
  • Lowest USD entry price with major-brand EU infrastructure
  • Strong API, Terraform, and Ansible support
  • High Frequency NVMe instances for latency-sensitive applications

Cons:

  • US-headquartered: CLOUD Act applies to EU-stored data
  • No formal uptime SLA with service credits on standard plans
  • USD billing only

Best for: Developers and global SaaS teams that need fine-grained EU latency control across nine cities and can accept CLOUD Act applicability.

6

Contabo Best Budget European VPS

Contabo is a German provider with 225,000+ customers and data centers in Munich and Nuremberg. It delivers the most RAM per euro of any established provider on this list and holds prices stable through mid-2026. Independent monitoring recorded 91.11% actual uptime over a 90-day window in early 2026, below its stated claim.

Starting from: ~€4.50/month

Pros:

  • German-owned, subject to EU law only, no CLOUD Act
  • Highest specs per euro ratio on this list
  • Stable pricing through mid-2026, no 2026 price increases
  • AMD EPYC processors are available
  • Zero setup fees

Cons:

  • Independent monitoring recorded 91.11% actual uptime in early 2026 (per portalzine.de analysis)
  • No formal uptime SLA with service credits
  • Ticket and email support only, slow response times documented under load
  • No crypto payments

Best for: Test environments, non-critical workloads, and budget-focused developers who need maximum specs at minimum cost and understand the uptime trade-off.

7

Hostinger Best Entry-Level EU VPS for Beginners

Hostinger serves European users through EU data centers in Vilnius (Lithuania), Amsterdam (Netherlands), and London (UK). Plans include AMD EPYC processors, NVMe SSD, KVM, and the hPanel control panel. Promotional pricing starts around $4.99/month. The renewal rate runs approximately five times the promotional rate, the largest renewal gap on this list.

Starting from: ~$4.99/month promotional; renewal substantially higher

Pros:

  • AMD EPYC and NVMe SSD at accessible entry pricing
  • hPanel simplifies management for non-technical users
  • Three EU city data center options
  • Lithuanian-headquartered parent, no CLOUD Act

Cons:

  • Renewal pricing is approximately 5x the promotional rate
  • No formal uptime SLA with service credits on standard plans
  • No dedicated vCPU on entry plans

Best for: Beginners and small businesses that want a simple control panel, NVMe performance, and an accessible entry price while understanding the renewal pricing reality.

Choosing the Right Provider

Need a formal uptime SLA: Atal Networks (99.99% + 100% service credit guarantee) and UpCloud (99.999% on premium plans) are the only two providers on this list with formal SLA documentation. OVHcloud adds a 99.95% Public Cloud SLA.

Need DDoS protection: OVHcloud's VAC system is the European standard for gaming and high-traffic public-facing platforms. Atal Networks includes DDoS protection on all plans.

Need EU data sovereignty without the CLOUD Act: All providers except Vultr are European-headquartered and subject to EU law only.

Need the lowest price: Vultr at $2.50/month leads on USD entry. UpCloud at €3/month leads among European-owned options.

Need Windows VPS: Atal Networks and Contabo both support Windows Server on EU VPS plans. Hetzner does not officially support Windows on standard cloud plans.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does hosting on a European VPS guarantee GDPR compliance?

No. Hosting inside the EU puts your data inside EU jurisdiction, supporting data residency requirements. GDPR compliance still requires a signed data processing agreement and documented breach notification procedures. See gdpr.eu for the full framework.

Which European VPS has the best uptime record?

Atal Networks backs every plan with a 99.99% uptime SLA and a 100% formal guarantee with service credits. UpCloud guarantees 99.999% on premium plans. Independent monitoring recorded 91.11% actual uptime for Contabo in early 2026. Always check third-party monitoring data alongside provider marketing claims.

Which European VPS providers accept cryptocurrency?

Atal Networks accepts crypto, PayPal, credit cards, and bank transfers. Vultr accepts crypto on select payment methods. Hetzner, OVHcloud, UpCloud, Contabo, and Hostinger do not list crypto as a standard payment option.

Final Recommendation

For European teams that need a formal 99.99% SLA with service credits, EU locations, Linux and Windows options, 24/7/365 support, and crypto payments, Atal Networks delivers that combination from $5.25/month. No other provider on this list matches all five criteria together.

For price-to-hardware value, Hetzner remains the European benchmark after the 2026 price increases. For DDoS-protected enterprise infrastructure, OVHcloud leads. For top disk benchmark performance with formal SLA documentation, UpCloud is the strongest EU-sovereign option.
