Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about placing orders, milestone security, and delivery.

How does the escrow protection work on Upwork and Fiverr?

When you purchase a project or gig, your payment is placed safely into platform escrow. A1 Technovation only receives the funds once we complete all agreed deliverables and you review and formally approve the milestone.

What if I need a custom package not listed in the catalog?

You can message us directly on Upwork, Fiverr, or WhatsApp with your exact requirements. We will prepare and submit a custom milestone proposal tailored specifically to your website and marketing goals.

Are revisions included in the gig deliverables?

Yes. Every gig and project includes revision rounds. If any implementation, audit detail, or configuration requires adjustment to meet the agreed scope, we update it promptly at no additional cost.

How do I provide site access securely?

Once you place your order, you can grant delegate or user access via Google Search Console, Google Tag Manager, Google Ads, Meta Business Manager, or WordPress admin using standard invite permissions without sharing root master passwords.

Can we transition from Upwork or Fiverr to a direct agency retainer?

Yes, while fully respecting platform terms of service. For long-term enterprise retainers, clients often start with a defined project on Upwork or Fiverr to verify our work, then proceed with comprehensive multi-month engagements.

What is your response time after placing an order?

We typically review orders and confirm requirements within 1 to 4 hours during business days. For urgent technical fixes (such as severe Search Console crawl blocks or broken tracking), work begins immediately upon access confirmation.
