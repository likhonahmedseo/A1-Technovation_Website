How We Compared These Providers

We looked for public web-hosting offers relevant to Taiwan, then reviewed the hosting type, location evidence, software support, account limits, backups, and billing terms.

Some providers publish detailed plan tables. Others need a quote or a written answer about the selected service. We identify those gaps rather than treating missing information as proof of poor service.

We also separate website hosting from server rental. A shared-hosting account usually comes with a configured web environment. A VPS provides more control, but its price may not include a control-panel license, website maintenance, email, or backup management.

That distinction can affect your costs more than a small difference in the advertised rental price.

How to Compare Taiwan Web Hosting Plans

Check the Origin Location and Website Response

The origin is the server that holds and runs the website. A CDN may deliver cached files from a closer location, while login, search, and other uncached requests still reach the origin.

Ask where the selected plan runs. Then test both cached and uncached pages from the networks your visitors use. Include mobile connections and repeat the checks at different times.

Server location is only part of the result. Image size, plugins, database queries, caching, and account limits also affect response time. Do not assume that moving a slow website to Taiwan will fix every performance problem.

Confirm WordPress and Database Support

At the time of research, WordPress.org recommended PHP 8.3 or later, MariaDB 10.11 or later or MySQL 8.0 or later, and HTTPS.[10]

Ask the provider for the versions available on your actual plan. Check whether your theme and plugins support them. A product page that lists old versions needs clarification before purchase.

Also separate installation from management. One-click WordPress installation does not establish who handles updates, broken plugins, malware cleanup, or failed upgrades.

Read the Limits Behind Disk Space

A large storage allowance does not show how many busy requests an account can process. Check these critical limits:

CPU & Memory Allocation Memory limits determine whether PHP can render large WooCommerce catalogs.
PHP Workers & Processes Limits how many simultaneous uncached checkout requests the site processes.
Database Size & Connections Max connections prevent MySQL crashes during peak traffic sales.
File Count (Inode Allowance) WordPress image thumbs and caches rapidly consume strict inode quotas.
Monthly Transfer & Overages Check whether accounts throttle bandwidth or assess unexpected overage fees.
Mailbox Storage & Sending Hourly SMTP limits affect transaction emails, invoice delivery, and newsletters.

Ask what happens when a limit is reached. The provider may throttle requests, charge an overage, or require an upgrade. Shared account CPU limits should not be read as a promise of exclusive physical cores.

Check Backups by the Restore Process

A backup feature is useful only if you can recover the right data when needed.

Ask what gets copied, how often, how long copies remain available, and where they are stored. Confirm whether you can restore files and databases separately, and whether support charges for help.

For WordPress, test files and the database together. For an online store, consider how many orders could be lost between backup points. A daily copy may not meet your business’s recovery needs.

Keep a usable copy outside the hosting account where practical. Do not assume an account backup survives every server or account failure.

Match Support to the Work You Need Done

Record the support language, channel, hours, and duties. A Traditional Chinese control panel does not prove that every technical-support channel operates in Traditional Chinese.

Ask a specific question before purchase: “If a WordPress update breaks checkout, will you restore the site, troubleshoot the plugin, or ask us to contact our developer?”

The answer helps separate infrastructure support from application maintenance. Also ask who you contact outside normal business hours and what access they need to help.

How to Move Your Website Without Losing Data or Email

A migration should have a tested destination and a rollback route. Follow these sequential steps:

1
Complete Pre-Migration Backup Back up the website files, database, and any email you need to move to an offsite location.
2
Copy & Staging Validation Copy the site to the new host and test it thoroughly through a preview address or local hosts-file override.
3
Comprehensive Functionality Testing Check user login, contact forms, image rendering, search, checkout, HTTPS certificates, and cron scheduled tasks.
4
Final Database Synchronization Plan the final database transfer during off-peak hours so new orders or customer submissions are not overwritten by an older copy.
5
Cutover DNS Records Carefully Change the required web A/AAAA DNS records. Keep existing MX and SPF mail records intact if business email is staying elsewhere.
6
Parallel Monitoring & Decommission Monitor the new service and keep the old hosting environment active until all DNS TTL caches expire and checks pass.

Ask the new host which steps its migration service covers. “Free migration” may not include email, custom applications, or troubleshooting code. Do not cancel the previous service before confirming that the new site and mail flow work.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Taiwan Web Hosting Provider Is Best for a Small Business?
Choose according to the website software and the help your team needs. Shared hosting may suit a small company site. Atal Networks is our featured option when server control matters and someone can manage the environment. We have not run tests that establish one provider as best for every business.
Is Taiwan Hosting the Same as Hosting for Taiwan Visitors?
No. A service can target Taiwan customers while running its origin servers in another country. Ask for the physical location of the selected plan. A Taiwan-language website, Taiwan IP address, or nearby CDN edge does not by itself confirm where the origin server runs.
Can We Run WordPress on an Atal Networks Taiwan VPS?
A suitable Linux VPS can run WordPress after an administrator installs a compatible web server, PHP, database, and HTTPS setup. Confirm the selected VPS resources and management scope. Do not assume that WordPress setup, updates, email, and backups come with the VPS rental.
Do Taiwan Web Hosting Plans Include Email and SSL?
Some shared plans include mailboxes and certificate tools, while other services charge separately or expect you to configure them. Check mailbox capacity, sending limits, certificate renewal, and support. Website hosting and business email can also remain with separate providers if that better fits your needs.
Is Shared Hosting Enough for WooCommerce?
It can be enough for a store whose resource needs fit the plan. Test uncached pages, checkout, scheduled tasks, and plugin behavior. Confirm correct cache exclusions and a recovery process that protects order data. Choose from measured demand rather than a fixed monthly-visitor rule.
Can We Keep Our Domain with Another Registrar?
Yes. The domain registrar and website host do not have to be the same company. Keep control of the domain account and update the required DNS records. Check mail and verification records before changing nameservers so you do not interrupt services that should remain elsewhere.
When Should We Move from Shared Hosting to a VPS?
Consider a VPS when you need software or settings the shared plan does not allow, or when repeated resource limits affect a well-maintained site. Include administration, backups, and licenses in the cost. A VPS gives more control, but it also needs someone to manage it.
