Technical SEO

Build the Foundation That Lets Everything Else Work

Technical SEO determines whether Google can find, crawl, understand, and index your renovation website at all. If the site is slow, confusing, duplicated, or difficult to crawl, even strong copy and beautiful project photography will not produce stable rankings.

  • Site speed and Core Web Vitals: we identify image, template, and script bottlenecks that slow down portfolio-heavy renovation websites and hurt both rankings and form conversions.
  • Mobile-first optimization: homeowners compare contractors on their phones, so we ensure project galleries, service sections, and quote forms remain fast and easy to use.
  • Crawl structure: we fix broken links, redirect chains, duplicate pages, indexation issues, and weak page hierarchy that stop Google from understanding your services.
  • Structured data: we implement Service, FAQPage, LocalBusiness, and review-ready schema so your content is eligible for richer search presentation.
  • Security and discoverability: HTTPS, XML sitemap, robots directives, and canonical controls all get aligned so Google indexes the right version of every page.

Most renovation websites are built around project visuals and contact forms rather than search architecture. That means we routinely find 15 to 25 technical issues that actively suppress rankings before content strategy even begins.

Local SEO

Rank in the Map Pack for Every City You Serve

For renovation companies, the Google Map Pack often produces the highest-converting inbound calls of any organic surface. Homeowners searching for a contractor in a specific city are already near the decision stage.

  • Google Business Profile optimization: correct categories, service areas, photos, Q&A, and business detail alignment.
  • NAP consistency: standardized name, address, and phone data across contractor, local, and review directories.
  • Local citation building: relevant directory consistency across trusted platforms that reinforce renovation industry credibility.
  • Review strategy: a repeatable process for capturing more 5-star Google reviews from completed projects.
  • Service area pages: dedicated pages for the cities and suburbs you serve without creating duplicate content problems.

If your company serves multiple cities, counties, or metro areas, we build a scalable service-area structure that captures local search demand across the full footprint while preserving quality and relevance.

Frequently Asked Questions

SEO for Renovation Companies Questions Answered

How long does SEO take for a renovation company?
A renovation company new to SEO typically sees its first measurable organic lead increases between months 3 and 6. Competitive head-term rankings like "kitchen remodeling company [major city]" usually take 6 to 12 months. Map Pack improvements often start showing within 60 to 90 days when local fundamentals are addressed early.
How much does renovation company SEO cost?
Most renovation company campaigns at A1 Technovation fall between 800 and 3500 USD per month depending on market size, number of services, competitive pressure, and whether local SEO, content, and link building all need to scale together. We scope pricing after the free audit instead of forcing every company into the same package.
Do renovation companies need local SEO or national SEO?
Most need both. Local SEO helps you win "near me" and city-specific commercial searches from homeowners ready to hire. Broader content SEO and topical authority make those local pages stronger and more resilient over time.
What renovation keywords should my company target?
The highest-value keywords usually combine service, location, and intent. Examples include "kitchen remodeling company [city]," "bathroom renovation contractor [suburb]," "home addition builders [metro area]," and "renovation company near me." Research-stage terms like "kitchen remodel cost [city]" also matter because they attract homeowners before they start calling around.
Will SEO help my renovation company get cited by ChatGPT and Gemini?
Yes, when the content is structured correctly. Our GEO work is designed around AI retrieval: direct answers, named entities, strong factual density, external trust references, and service pages that clearly associate your business with target renovation services and markets.
Can SEO replace Google Ads for my renovation company?
SEO and Google Ads play different roles. Ads generate immediate traffic while SEO builds long-term, compounding demand. For most renovation companies, the strongest model is to run both until organic visibility is strong enough to reduce dependence on paid clicks.
Does A1 Technovation work with renovation companies outside the US?
Yes. We work with renovation and home improvement companies across the US, UK, Canada, Australia, and other English-speaking markets. The core framework stays consistent, but keyword research, competition, and citation strategy are always localized to the market.
How does A1 Technovation report on SEO performance?
Every client receives clear reporting on rankings, organic impressions and clicks, Google Business Profile performance, content production, link acquisition, and the strategic next steps. We translate technical work into business impact instead of burying you under vanity metrics.
