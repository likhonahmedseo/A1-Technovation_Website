Technical SEO

Fix the Foundation Before You Build on It

Technical SEO is the layer most Toronto renovation sites skip, but it is the layer that determines whether Google can even find, trust, and rank your content properly.

  • Slow portfolio-heavy pages: renovation websites often lead with giant images that load poorly on mobile and drive bounce rates up before homeowners even reach the contact form.
  • Missing schema markup: no Service schema, LocalBusiness schema, FAQ schema, or review-ready markup means less eligibility for richer search treatment and weaker AI parsing.
  • Crawl and indexation problems: misconfigured robots, weak internal linking, and canonical conflicts leave revenue pages outside Google's best view of the site.
  • Non-mobile-first architecture: renovation buyers compare contractors on phones, not just desktops, so poor mobile experience directly damages rankings and conversion rate.
  • Duplicate city content: GTA businesses often repeat the same copy across local pages, which weakens every page instead of helping them all.

We fix the quick wins first, then the structural issues that determine whether the site can scale across Toronto and the GTA without performance or duplication problems.
