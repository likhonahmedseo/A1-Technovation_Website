How we selected Taiwan VPS providers

We looked for official plan pages or order catalogs that place the VPS in Taiwan. We compared the listed location, server resources, network limits, billing terms, and support options. We use the city only where the provider identifies it for the plan or region. Ask for the exact city when the provider lists only Taiwan.

A Taiwan IP address can help if your service needs a local IP. Check the VM location separately. For cost, compare the first invoice and the renewal for the configuration you need. An hourly VPS, a monthly plan, and a three-year prepayment can suit different buyers even when the advertised monthly figures look close.

To compare two quotes, write down the amount due now, the billing period, and the renewal. Add required IPv4 addresses, backups, licenses, and expected transfer charges. Keep prices in the original currency until you have a dated exchange rate and know what your payment provider charges.

Check the network from your users' side

  • Ask for a test IP or test URL on the exact plan and carrier.
  • Check latency and packet loss from networks your users rely on. Repeat the test during peak hours.
  • Test a real request or download. A ping result helps you judge the route. It does not measure how long a web page or API call takes.
  • Compare the port speed with the transfer allowance. Port speed tells you how fast the connection can send data. Transfer tells you how much data the plan includes over the billing period.

For a game server, check latency, jitter, and packet loss from player networks. For a store or API, check response times from your main customer locations. For mainland China traffic, test from the relevant ISPs before committing. If the VM must stay in Taiwan, check the location of backups separately.

Confirm support and recovery

Root access lets you control the server. On a self-managed plan, budget time for updates, firewall rules, monitoring, and restores. If you want a provider to handle those tasks, ask for a written support scope.

Check whether the backup is a snapshot, an exportable copy, or a service that includes restore help. Test a restore before you depend on it. For a short project, the ability to end billing may matter more than a low advertised price on a long term.

Taiwan VPS hosting FAQs

How much does a Taiwan VPS cost per month?
It depends on the resources and billing term. The sampled monthly offers include US$11 for LuckVM's 1 GB profile, US$20 for SoftShellWeb's 2 GB tier, and US$28 for Yuan-Jhen's 2 GB tier. Atal's US$5.21 monthly equivalent uses a three-year term. Compare the first invoice and renewal before choosing.
Can I get a Taiwan IP address with a Taipei VPS?
Yes. Several providers list a Taiwan IPv4 address on Taipei plans. Confirm that the address is included, check how the service you use classifies it, and confirm the physical VM location separately.
Is a Taipei VPS faster than a Tokyo or Hong Kong VPS for Taiwan users?
A Taipei VPS can reduce the network distance to Taiwan users. The route and ISP still affect latency. Use the same test from the same access network for each candidate before deciding.
Which Taiwan VPS plans support Windows?
IMIDC lists Windows on TW-Cloud-02, and EstNOC lists Windows on tiers above its Linux starter plan. Confirm the license fee, supported version, and minimum resources for your application before ordering.
Can I test latency before buying a Taiwan VPS?
Some providers publish test IPs, such as Serverfield for its Taichung HiNet product. Ask the provider for an endpoint on the exact network you intend to buy. If no endpoint is available, compare a short billing term or request a trial where offered.
