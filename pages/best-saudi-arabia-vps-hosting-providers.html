10 Best Saudi Arabia VPS Hosting Providers Compared

A Saudi Arabia VPS should meet three needs before you compare the price. It should run in the location your application requires, provide enough resources for the workload, and give you a clear bill for the period you plan to use it.

Atal Networks is our featured option for a Riyadh Linux VPS with two listed IPv4 addresses. LightNode suits buyers by comparing hourly deployments in Riyadh and Jeddah. For applications that need a wider cloud platform, Oracle, Google Cloud, and SCCC offer Saudi compute options with different purchasing and billing models.

This guide compares VPS packages and configurable cloud virtual machines. It explains which costs, location details, and support terms to check for each.

How we compared Saudi VPS hosting

We reviewed official product pages, location information, and relevant purchase documentation. The main checks were physical deployment, resource allocation, billing term, traffic charges, operating systems, support, and recovery.

A provider can serve Saudi customers through infrastructure in another country. For a workload that requires Saudi deployment, ask for the selected VM's location in the order or service agreement. A local IP address and a local VM are separate details to verify.

The list also includes three cloud-compute options. These can provide a virtual server alongside databases, storage, and other platform services. Compare their complete configuration against a VPS package, including public IPs, disks, backups, and outbound traffic.

The 10 Saudi Arabia VPS hosting providers

1. Atal Networks for a Riyadh Linux VPS with two IPv4 addresses

Featured Option Riyadh, Saudi Arabia
From US$5.21/mo

Atal Networks deserves a look if your application needs a Linux VPS in Riyadh with more than one public IPv4 address. Its VPS Start listing includes one CPU core, 2 GB RAM, 25 GB disk, 5 TB of transfer, KVM virtualization, and root access.

Two addresses can serve separate public endpoints on the same server. The 5 TB allowance is also a useful comparison point when a project sends regular downloads or backups. Confirm how the plan counts traffic and what happens if you exceed it.

The displayed US$5.21 monthly equivalent requires US$187.50 paid every three years. Request the current month-to-month price, renewal terms, Riyadh stock, and included port speed before ordering. For a new workload, a shorter billing term can give you time to check the route and resource use before making a longer commitment. Atal Networks Riyadh VPS hosting plans

2. LightNode for hourly Riyadh and Jeddah deployments

Hourly & Dual-City Riyadh & Jeddah, Saudi Arabia
From $0.012/hr

LightNode publishes VPS locations in both Riyadh and Jeddah. That makes it a candidate for temporary staging, development, or testing the same application from two Saudi locations. Its Saudi VPS page lists Linux and Windows images, root access, NVMe storage, and hourly billing.

The page advertises US$7.71 per month and US$0.012 per hour. Those figures need a billing explanation because US$0.012 multiplied by 730 hours is US$8.76. Confirm any monthly cap or discount in the console for the selected city.

Also check whether stopped instances retain billable storage or IPs. Use the exact VPS product when comparing plans because the provider's VDS page publishes a separate offer. LightNode Saudi VPS

3. UltaHost for buyers who want managed VPS support

Managed Hosting Saudi Arabia
From US$5.52/mo

UltaHost's Saudi page lists a managed VPS Basic plan with one CPU core, 1 GB RAM, 30 GB NVMe storage, a dedicated IPv4 address, and IPv6 addresses. The observed offer displays US$5.52 per month and a two-year term.

It is worth considering if you want help operating a small server. Ask which tasks the managed service covers, including OS updates, control-panel issues, backup restores, and application errors. Put those duties in writing before comparing it with a self-managed VPS.

The entry tier has 1 GB RAM. Check the memory needs of your application and control panel, then price the required tier. Confirm the Saudi city and full initial invoice at checkout. UltaHost Saudi VPS

How much does a Saudi Arabia VPS cost?

Published entry prices can help you form a shortlist, but the invoice and configuration determine the useful comparison.

Atal's featured rate requires a three-year payment. UltaHost's observed entry offer uses a two-year term and a smaller memory allocation. LightNode offers hourly billing with advertised monthly figures that should be checked against the selected plan.

For a business website or API, ask each provider to price the same starting requirement. For example, use 2 GB RAM, at least 40 GB usable disk, one public IPv4 address, a defined monthly outbound allowance, and a daily backup. Mark any upgrade needed to meet those requirements.

Riyadh, Jeddah, or Dammam?

Choose a city using the networks that reach your application and the services it depends on. A Riyadh office accessing an internal system has a different traffic pattern from an online store serving customers across the country.

Request a test IP or URL for the exact location. Check from your main customer networks, such as relevant STC, Mobily, or Zain connections. Repeat during busy and quiet periods.

Measure both the route and the application. Ping helps show round-trip time. An HTTP request shows more of what a visitor experiences. For a game or other real-time service, also check packet loss and jitter.

Keep databases and application servers close enough to meet your response-time needs. If you plan to use a cloud database, confirm its regional availability before choosing the VM location.

What to check before ordering

Port speed and transfer allowance

A port rate and a monthly transfer allowance describe different limits. A 1 Gbps port may support a short burst of traffic, while a transfer allowance limits how much data the plan includes over the billing period.

Ask whether the port is shared, what traffic counts toward the allowance, and what happens at the limit. Include off-site backups in your estimate if outbound backup traffic is chargeable.

Operating system and administration

A Windows image may carry a license cost. A control panel may need another license and more RAM. Confirm both on the selected configuration.

For Linux, check the supported images, root access, reinstall process, and recovery console. Ask whether “managed” includes OS patching, firewall changes, application troubleshooting, and restores. Use the written scope to decide how much work your team must handle.

Backup location and recovery

Ask where backups are stored, how many copies are retained, and whether you can download one. Check whether a restore replaces the whole VM or lets you recover individual files.

If a daily backup is enough, record that decision. If losing a day's orders would be unacceptable, plan a more frequent database backup. Test a restore before relying on the service.

Where your data and access reside

Record the location of the VM, backups, logs, and any separate database. Ask how support staff access the system and where those support services operate.

For an organization with specific contractual or regulatory requirements, have the responsible team review the provider's current documents against the actual workload. The purchase decision should cover every service that stores or processes the data.

Frequently asked questions

Which providers offer servers inside Saudi Arabia?
The providers in this guide publish Saudi hosting, VPS, or compute offerings. Atal names Riyadh, LightNode names Riyadh and Jeddah, Oracle lists both cities, and Google Cloud documents Dammam. Confirm the deployment location of the exact product you order.
Can I get a Saudi VPS with a local IP address?
Providers such as LightNode advertise a local Saudi IP. Ask whether the selected IP is dedicated, check how relevant services locate it, and confirm reverse DNS support if required. Verify the VM location separately.
Which Saudi VPS plans support Windows?
LightNode and UltaHost publish Windows options. Other providers may offer Windows on selected configurations or by quote. Confirm the license cost, supported version, memory requirements, and remote-access method before buying.
Can I pay monthly instead of annually?
Billing terms vary by provider and plan. Ask for a monthly invoice for the exact location and configuration. A price shown as “per month” can be the average cost of a longer prepaid contract.
Is Riyadh faster than Jeddah for Saudi users?
It depends on the source network, routing, and application. Test the exact endpoints from the networks your users rely on. Repeat at different times before deciding which location performs better for them.
Does a Saudi VPS include backups?
It depends on the plan. Check whether backups are included or optional, where they are stored, and how you restore them. Ask whether you can export a copy to storage under your own control.
