10 Best Dedicated Servers in Singapore (2026)

Physical Singapore data centers, hardware generation, formal SLA terms, and PDPA compliance compared across 10 providers. All pricing in USD.

Singapore dedicated server searches return aggregator lists ranked by ad budget and single-provider self-promotional pages. Most do not name the actual data center facility, skip Singapore's PDPA compliance requirements, and miss why Singapore sits at the center of ASEAN routing.

This guide covers 10 providers, reviewed on four criteria: physical Singapore data center presence, hardware generation, formal SLA terms, and PDPA compliance posture. All pricing in USD. Atal Networks ranks first. No paid placements, no affiliate rankings.

Singapore as a Dedicated Server Location

Singapore is Southeast Asia's primary internet hub. Multiple submarine cable systems connect it to Europe, the US, Australia, India, Japan, and China. No other ASEAN city matches that density.

Equinix SG1 at 20 Ayer Rajah Crescent is the most carrier-connected data center in Asia-Pacific. Equinix SG3 sits across the street. Keppel DC (Genting Lane campus), Nxera, AirTrunk SGP1 and SGP2, and Global Switch at Tai Seng all operate in Singapore. Singapore hosts approximately 45 data centers, with Equinix SG6 (20 MW) and Keppel DC SGP 9 both targeting 2026 completion.

Singapore vs. Hong Kong

Singapore suits ASEAN-primary workloads, international businesses needing political stability, and organizations subject to Singapore PDPA compliance. Hong Kong delivers 5 to 10ms to Shenzhen and Guangdong, making it the better choice for China-primary workloads. For most international businesses, Singapore is the correct primary APAC location. Hong Kong works as a secondary node for China-specific traffic.

PDPA Compliance: What Singapore Server Buyers Need to Know

The PDPA, enforced by the PDPC at pdpc.gov.sg, governs personal data handling for Singapore residents. Hosting in Singapore supports data residency without extra contractual mechanisms. Since October 2020, organizations must notify the PDPC within 3 business days of a qualifying data breach. Your provider must have documented incident response procedures that meet this timeline.

Before signing, confirm: physical Singapore server location, ISO 27001 or SOC 2 Type II certification, data processing agreement availability, and PDPA-aligned breach notification procedures.

Our Evaluation Criteria

Physical Singapore data center. We confirmed which providers run hardware inside Singapore specifically, not "APAC" routing from Malaysia.

Hardware generation. Intel Xeon Scalable and AMD EPYC are the 2026 standard. NVMe SSD is the baseline. ECC RAM matters for databases.

Formal SLA vs. marketing claim. A 99.99% uptime headline without service credits carries no contractual protection. We separated formal SLAs from marketing claims.

Pricing in USD. Entry-level Singapore dedicated servers start around $75 to $150/month. Mid-range AMD EPYC or Xeon Scalable with NVMe runs $200 to $400/month.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the PDPA and does it affect dedicated server hosting in Singapore?

The PDPA, enforced by the PDPC at pdpc.gov.sg, governs personal data handling for Singapore residents. Hosting in Singapore supports PDPA data residency. Organizations must still maintain data processing agreements, implement reasonable security controls, and notify PDPC within 3 business days of a qualifying data breach.

Which Singapore dedicated server providers have a formal uptime SLA?

Atal Networks backs every Singapore plan with a 99.99% uptime SLA and a 100% formal guarantee with service credits. Gcore documents formal SLA terms. OVHcloud carries formal SLAs on Advance and Scale dedicated tiers. Vultr, Hetzner, Contabo, and Cherry Servers do not publish formal SLAs with service credits on standard dedicated plans.

Should I choose Singapore or Hong Kong for my APAC dedicated server?

Singapore for ASEAN-primary workloads, stable regulatory environment, and PDPA compliance. Hong Kong for China-primary workloads (5 to 10ms to Shenzhen vs. 30 to 40ms from Singapore). Most international businesses use Singapore as the primary APAC node.

Can I get a 10Gbps dedicated server in Singapore?

Yes. Atal Networks offers 10Gbps unmetered dedicated server options in Singapore. Vultr offers up to 25Gbps bare metal ports. OVHcloud and Liquid Web also provide high-bandwidth configurations.

Which Singapore dedicated server providers accept cryptocurrency?

Atal Networks accepts crypto, PayPal, credit card, and bank transfer. Cherry Servers accepts 20+ payment methods including cryptocurrency. Most other providers on this list limit payment to card and bank transfer.
